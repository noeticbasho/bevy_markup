## Symptoms

`<path d='M0 0 A1e30 1e30 0 1 1 1e30 0'/>` took 4.3 s to convert (ASan
build). With `A3e38 …`, and 100 such arcs, it ran past 100 s.
`<circle r='3e38'/>` took 0.3 s per circle.

## Root cause

svgtypes' `SimplifyingPathParser` converts an arc with kurbo's
`to_cubic_beziers(0.1, …)`. The tolerance is absolute, so an arc of radius
`r` becomes about `(11.16·r)^⅙` cubics per turn: 1.5·10⁵ at `r = 1e30`.
The simplifier pushes them all into a `Vec`, then hands them out with
`Vec::remove(0)`, quadratic in the count. Shapes go through tiny-skia's
`arc_to` with the same tolerance: linear, but just as many cubics.

## Fix

The bound counts each path's segments without generating them. It walks
svgtypes' raw `PathParser`, tracks the current point as the simplifier
does, and computes each arc's cubics with kurbo's arithmetic. The work
adds 4 units per segment and (cubics)²/64 per arc for the drain. Shape
radii resolve as usvg resolves lengths (`em`/`ex` against the inherited
font size, `%` against the inherited viewport, absolute units at 96 dpi).
