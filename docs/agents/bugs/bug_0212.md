## Symptoms

After an `.svg` is modified while a raster of it is being drawn, the
span keeps showing the old drawing until its displayed size changes.
Reachable through hot reload and `sync_svg_fonts`' reload of documents
with `<text>` only.

## Root cause

On `AssetEvent::Modified`, `rasterize_svg_images` drops the document's
`ready` and `refused` entries but not `drawing` (`svg.rs:1140-1143`). A
task started on the old tree finishes into `ready` under the same key.

## Fix proposed by the review

Drop (or generation-tag) the `drawing` entries on `Modified` too, so a
stale task's result is discarded.

## Fix

On a document's `Modified`, `rasterize_svg_images` also drops its
entries from `SvgRasters::drawing` (dropping a `Task` cancels it).
