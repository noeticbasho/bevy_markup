## Symptoms

Release build, Bevy's default font, one `<text>` of N characters at
10 px; parse time only:

| N | `M` | a character the font lacks |
|---|---|---|
| 20 000 | 173 ms | 548 ms |
| 100 000 | 3.93 s | 13.5 s |

Five times the text costs 23–25 times the time. Both are inside the
work budget (the larger is 57% of it). 20 000 separate one-letter
`<text>` elements parse in 104 ms, so the cost is per text chunk.
Present at `f76b320`.

## Root cause

`svg_bounds` charges 16 units per text byte, linearly. usvg's text
layout is quadratic in a chunk's length; where in usvg isn't located
yet (UPSTREAM U41).

## Fix

Located by reading usvg 0.49 (UPSTREAM U41, U43): `code_point_at`
counts from the chunk's start once per cluster (characters²), and every
span reshapes the whole chunk and merges its glyphs by a linear search
(spans × characters; 4 000 one-character `<tspan>`s took 2.0 s, which the
review didn't probe). The missing-character slowdown is the same
quadratic with multi-byte characters, not a second one.
`Graph::text_work` charges a `<text>` by characters² ÷ 32 plus spans ×
characters × 2.
