## Symptoms

One 16 × 16 `icon.svg`, N `<img>`s with `style='width: 1500px'`,
`1501px`, …; the bytes of `Assets<Image>`:

| `<img>`s | page | image bytes |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 175 B | 36.1 MB |
| 8 | 343 B | 72.3 MB |
| 16 | 679 B | 145.4 MB |

9 MB each here, 64 MB each at 4096 px: 100 tags (4 KB of template) are
6.4 GB, each also a concurrent task holding a pixmap and a copy.

## Root cause

`MAX_SVG_SIDE` and `MAX_SVG_COST` bound one drawing. `SvgRasters` keeps
one raster per (document, displayed size) in use, and `InlineSvgs` one
per distinct text for 120 frames; the page chooses how many.
(`canvas.rs` refuses a single 100 000 px canvas for this reason.)

## Fix proposed by the review

A byte budget over `SvgRasters` and the inline cache; past it, a span
keeps its 1× image (what a non-SVG image gets). Whether this is owed
depends on the threat model for templates (bug_0220).

## Fix

Templates are untrusted (ADR 0044), so the page's choice of sizes is
bounded: `svg::MAX_SVG_RASTER_BYTES` (256 MiB) per cache.
`rasterize_svg_images` adds up the ready and in-flight redraws
(`SvgRasters::used_bytes`) and starts a draw only if it fits; a span
over the budget keeps its 1× image and retries when rasters are dropped.
`InlineSvgs` reserves an inline image's bytes in a shared counter before
drawing (on the task too, once its size is known), releases them when
the entry is dropped or the draw fails, and remembers over-budget texts
until an entry is dropped.
