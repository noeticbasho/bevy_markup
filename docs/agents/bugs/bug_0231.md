# bug_0231 — The SVG `<style>` charge assumes cheap selectors, and refuses only after the matching ran

## Symptoms

Release build, `svg::parse`:

| rules × elements | document | result |
|---|---|---|
| 5 000 × 6 000, type selectors | 71 KB | loads, 299 ms |
| 30 000 × 1 000, type selectors | 315 KB | refused (`TooLarge`) after 3.25 s |
| 2 000 rules of 16 classes × 16 000 elements with 16 classes | 737 KB | refused (`TooLarge`) after 13.6 s |

The last is 3.2 × 10⁷ pairs, just under the early check, at about
400 ns a pair where the charge assumes 15.

## Root cause

bug_0207 charges rules × elements ÷ 4 and refuses early only when that
alone passes `MAX_SVG_WORK`. Below that, the matching runs and the total
is checked afterwards. The charge per pair is constant, but a rule's
cost per element grows with its selector's parts and classes.

## Fix (proposed)

Weigh each rule by its selector's parts and classes, and refuse before
matching when the weighted sum passes the budget. Together with
bug_0229's fix this is one bound on selector cost.
