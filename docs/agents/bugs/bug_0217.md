## Symptoms

`<canvas width='5000' height='100'>` then `<canvas width='60'
height='30'>`, both naming one camera with an 8 × 8 image: the first
shows `Nothing`, the second `Image` (stretched), and the image stays
8 × 8.

## Root cause

The `MAX_CANVAS_SIDE` gate runs after the owner is chosen. A canvas over
the limit that comes first in the document becomes the owner and shows
nothing.

## Fix (proposed)

Leave oversized canvases out of the owner candidates.
