## Symptoms

Release build, `svg::parse`; one `<style>` rule and nested `<g>`s around
a `<rect>`:

| `<g>` depth | selector | document | parse |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | `q g g g g` | 409 B | 9.6 ms |
| 40 | `q g g g g g g` | 413 B | 288 ms |
| 40 | `q g g g g g g g` | 415 B | 1.25 s |
| 60 | `q g g g g g g` | 553 B | 5.24 s |
| 60 | `q g g g g g g g` | 555 B | 35.7 s |

All five then load. One more part or a deeper nest multiplies the time.
For an inline `<svg>` up to 64 KB this runs during the build, on the
main thread: `parse_unless_costly` computes the bounds before it can
defer to a task. Present at `f76b320`; review_0007 K04 (bug_0207) looked
at this pass and missed it.

## Root cause

simplecss 0.2.2 matches a descendant combinator by trying every ancestor
and recursing (`selector.rs:174`), with no memo: a selector of k parts
over d nested elements costs about C(d, k) when its leftmost part never
matches. `svg_bounds::Graph::new` runs that match before any budget is
counted (bug_0207 charges rules × elements: 1 × 61 here), and usvg's
`resolve_css` runs it again.

## Fix

`svg_bounds::match_work` charges each `<style>` rule, before anything
matches, by simplecss's exact worst-case call count: Σ over elements of
Σ_{j≤k} C(depth, j) for k descendant combinators (`descendant_calls`,
over the document's depth histogram), times the cost of a call. The
review's 555-byte document is refused in microseconds; ordinary sheets
cost a few percent of `MAX_SVG_WORK`. UPSTREAM U40 has the matcher's
defect and a PR sketch. Probing also showed this pass isn't where the
time went: the model matches only rules carrying references or lengths,
and usvg's `resolve_css` matches every rule.
