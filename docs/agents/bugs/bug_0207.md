## Symptoms

R rules `qN{r:1}` over 4R `<g/>` elements, `svg::parse`, release:

| rules | elements | bytes | parse |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 000 | 8 000 | 50 974 | 90 ms |
| 4 000 | 16 000 | 102 974 | 363 ms |
| 8 000 | 32 000 | 206 974 | 1.50 s |

4× per doubling. A 1 MB file is ~37 s, inside `MAX_SVG_WORK` (32 000
elements are 6% of it); ADR 0040 promises ~0.5 s at the limit.

## Root cause

`Graph::new` matches every kept rule against every element
(`svg_bounds.rs:387`); usvg's `resolve_css` does the same, and
`svg_boxes::open` a third time when the document has a filter, mask or
pattern. A rule that matches nothing adds no edge and no declaration,
so `MAX_REFERENCES` never counts it, and elements cost 16 units of work
each whatever the sheet holds.

## Fix proposed by the review

Add rules × elements to `Bounds::work` (both are known before the loop
runs), or cap the product.

## Fix

`svg_bounds::bounds` takes the work budget. `Graph::new` counts rules ×
elements (every rule, as usvg matches every rule) ÷
`RULE_MATCHES_PER_WORK` (4: ~15 ns a pair against ~9 ns measured over
the three passes) and refuses with `TooLarge` *before* its own matching
pass when that alone is over the budget; the count is added to
`Bounds::work`. An editor's export (200 class rules over 5 000 elements)
stays at 3% of the budget.
