## Symptoms

A 4000 × 100 document of `<path d='M0 y H4000' stroke='black'
stroke-width='1' stroke-dasharray='0.0041'/>` (~490 000 dashes per path),
release build:

| paths | bytes | estimate | of the budget | draw |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 396 | 832 512 | 0.62% | 132 ms |
| 16 | 1 368 | 929 856 | 0.69% | 520 ms |
| 16, undashed | | | | 1.8 ms |

One path adds ~8 100 units and ~32 ms: 4 µs per unit against the
calibrated 3.8 ns. The budget admits ~16 000 such paths (a 1.4 MB file):
about 9 minutes of drawing.

## Root cause

The estimate charges a path its segments and its bounding box once per
paint; `stroke-dasharray` appears nowhere in `src/`. tiny-skia dashes at
draw time, up to 1 000 000 dashes per contour (`dash.rs`
`MAX_DASH_COUNT`), each stroked on its own. The calibration
(`adr-0038-probe.md` §3) only tried a dash so small that tiny-skia gives
up (`0.0001` over 10⁹ px).

## Fix (proposed)

Charge `min(length / interval, 10⁶)` dashes per contour (the length from
the path's bounds is an upper bound per segment), or refuse dash
intervals below a fraction of a pixel. Add `stroke-dasharray` to
`hostile_magnitudes_never_panic` and the calibration.
