## Symptoms

`TestUi::new`, release, `settle()`; N nested `<div>`s around one `<p>`;
the rule is `S p { color: red }` with `S` = `.never div` wrapped in
`:is(…) div` L times:

| depth | levels | CSS | HTML | settle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 80 | 2 | 68 B | 888 B | 8 ms |
| 80 | 3 | 77 B | 888 B | 24 ms |
| 80 | 4 | 86 B | 888 B | 266 ms |
| 95 | 4 | 86 B | 1 053 B | 623 ms |
| 95 | 5 | 95 B | 1 053 B | 9.3 s |
| 95 | 6 | 104 B | 1 053 B | **120.9 s** |

About 14× per level at depth 95; seven levels (what the paren guard
allows) would be near half an hour. The build runs on the main thread.

## Root cause

parcel's matcher is linear for a plain descendant chain: a failed walk
to the root returns `NotMatchedGlobally` and ends the match. An `:is()`
argument is matched by a call of its own whose failure is local, so the
outer loop moves to the next ancestor and calls it again; each level of
`:is(… div) div` multiplies by the remaining depth. ADR 0034's allowlist
admits descendant combinators inside `:is()`/`:where()`/`:not()`;
`MAX_CSS_PAREN_NESTING` (8) allows seven levels and `MAX_DOM_DEPTH` 100.
The testbench's `selector-bombs` nests `:not()` six deep (dropped) and
has no nested `:is()`.

## Fix (proposed)

In `Selector::from_parsed`, allow a combinator inside at most one level
of `:is()`/`:where()`/`:not()` (nested lists of compounds stay free), or
bound levels × combinators. Add a nested-`:is()` bomb to the testbench.
