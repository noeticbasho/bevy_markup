## Symptoms

Release build, settle time of one build; N copies of one rule, `plain` =
`.never div div`, `is` = `:is(.never div) div`:

| page | rules | `plain` | `is` |
|---|---|---|---|
| chains 95 deep | 100 | 22 ms | 162 ms |
| chains 95 deep | 400 (13 KB) | 33 ms | 599 ms |
| chains 48 deep | 400 | 17 ms | 92 ms |

That is 1.5 ms per rule at depth 95. Ten or twenty identical chains cost
the same (the style cache shares them); a second `:is()` in the rule
adds nothing. Pages with differing chains weren't measured.

## Root cause

bug_0213 allows a combinator inside one level of `:is()`. Each test of
such a compound walks to the root, and the enclosing descendant walk
tests it at every ancestor. `allowed_is_arguments_match_in_linear_time`
asserts at most 8× for 4× the depth, which this quadratic per element
plus fixed overhead passes.

## Fix

A sheet may hold `MAX_COMBINATOR_ARGUMENTS` (64) `:is()`/`:where()`/
`:not()` arguments with a combinator, across its rules; rules past it
are skipped and logged at `debug` like other unsupported selectors. 64
such rules cost about 0.1 s per build at the deepest allowed DOM. The
plain case (`.never div div`) is linear in the sheet's size and isn't
capped. The old scaling test's name and bound claimed linear time; it
now states and bounds the quadratic.
